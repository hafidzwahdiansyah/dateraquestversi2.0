📊 Iterative Dashboard

Iterative Dashboard merupakan kompetisi yang dirancang untuk menguji kemampuan peserta dalam mengolah data mentah menjadi informasi yang bernilai melalui dashboard interaktif, informatif, dan menarik secara visual. Peserta ditantang untuk melakukan proses pembersihan data, eksplorasi, analisis, hingga menyajikan hasil dalam bentuk visualisasi yang mudah dipahami sehingga mampu mendukung pengambilan keputusan yang tepat berdasarkan data.

Dalam kompetisi ini, peserta akan menggunakan dataset yang merepresentasikan permasalahan nyata dari berbagai bidang. Penilaian tidak hanya berfokus pada akurasi analisis, tetapi juga pada kualitas visualisasi, kreativitas desain dashboard, kemampuan menyampaikan insight, serta efektivitas komunikasi data. Melalui kompetisi ini, peserta diharapkan mampu mengembangkan keterampilan analitis, berpikir kritis, dan data storytelling yang menjadi kompetensi penting di era transformasi digital.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Find answers to the most common questions about DateraQuest 2.0, including eligibility, registration, payment, and competition rules.

Who can participate in DateraQuest 2.0?

DateraQuest 2.0 is open to active undergraduate students from universities across Indonesia. Participants must provide a valid student ID during registration.

What competition categories are available?

DateraQuest 2.0 consists of three competition categories: Iterative Dashboard, Machine Learning, and Essay Quest. Participants may choose the category that best matches their interests and expertise.

Can I participate individually or in a team?

Participation format depends on the competition category. Dashboard and Machine Learning are team competitions (2–3 members), while Essay Quest is an individual competition.

How do I complete my registration?

Create an account, complete the registration form, choose your competition category, make the registration payment, upload the payment proof, and wait for verification from the committee.

Where can I download the guidebook?

The official guidebook can be downloaded from the "Download Guidebook" button available on the homepage or through your participant dashboard after registration.

How can I contact the committee?

If you have any questions, you can contact the DateraQuest committee via WhatsApp, email, or our official Instagram account listed in the Contact section.
